# WildHatch guest expedition

Use only the visible WildHatch UI in the browser. Start with a fresh guest visit. Use the configured start URL and stay in WildHatch. No wallet extension or wallet credentials are needed.

## Check the signed-in mode gate

On the WildHatch title screen, open Normal mode. Verify that its dialog asks the player to connect a wallet and shows a **Connect wallet** button. Do not connect a wallet. Press **Back to main menu** to close the dialog and verify **Play as guest** is available.

## Enter the shared island

Click **Play as guest**. Allow the 3D loading screen to finish. Verify that the island opens with **GUEST MODE** visible in the field HUD and no wallet prompt. Verify that the **Open practice orbs** shortcut is available. Remain outside any encounter.

## Verify the new guest’s actual inventory

Use the **Open practice orbs** shortcut in the field HUD. Verify that the **Practice orbs** dialog says **3 of 3 Jade orbs remaining for this visit.** Verify that its instructions explain that practice catches release the beast and award no items or progress. Click **Continue exploring** and verify that the dialog closes and the island HUD remains visible.
